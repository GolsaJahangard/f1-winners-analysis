# Formula 1 Race Winners Analysis (1950-2025)

A comprehensive analysis of Formula 1 history through race winners, driver dominance, team performance, and global expansion of the sport.

## Dataset

- **1,142 races** from 1950 to 2025
- 9 columns: date, continent, grand_prix, circuit, winner_name, team, time, laps, year
